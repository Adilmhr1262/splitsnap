      About SplitSnap

      SplitSnap splits one shared bill between friends, family, or colleagues. It works for a meal, a trip, or any shared purchase.

      How to use it

      1. Enter the bill amount, currency, and number of people.
      2. Pick Equal split, or Custom split and type each person's base amount.
      3. Add a tip percentage and any fixed extra charges if needed.
      4. Press Calculate shares, then Copy summary to share the result, or Save to history to keep it.

      How the maths works

      All amounts are worked out in whole minor units (for example, paisa or cents). Tip is a percentage of the subtotal. In Custom mode, tip and extra charges are shared in proportion to each person's base amount. In Equal mode, they are shared evenly. Any leftover minor units from rounding are handed out one at a time, starting with the largest remainders, so the shares shown always add up exactly to the grand total.

      Privacy

      Your bills are stored only in this browser's local storage. There is no account, no server, and no tracking. Clearing your browser data removes your saved bills.

      What SplitSnap does not do

      It does not take payments, track expenses over time, send notifications, or sync to the cloud. It splits one bill at a time, and does that one job well.
